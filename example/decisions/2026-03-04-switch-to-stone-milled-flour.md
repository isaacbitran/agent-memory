**Decided:** Move bread flour from Northfield Milling to Ridgeline Mill, starting with the March 10 delivery.
**Why:** Local grain is part of what the bakery sells, Ridgeline delivers weekly, and Theo's test loaves had better flavor.
**Alternatives:** Stay with Northfield; blend the two flours; use Ridgeline only for the rye.
**Revisit if:** Ridgeline cannot cover the summer harvest gap, or the sourdough is still inconsistent after six weeks.
